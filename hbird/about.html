About HummingBird

Have you ever hated the fact that your favorite Website or other websites have so much ads? Well then HummingBird Browser is for you! Or if your just tired of AI being forced into your Web Browser well then HummingBird is also for you! Or if you just want a Simple Web Browser well then HummingBird is also for you!

HummingBird is designed with Speed in mind and is very Simple to use. Like, HummingBird has Tabs and buttons that do really what you want and not AI that gets in your way.

The default Search Engine used on the HummingBird Browser Homepage is powered by Google Programmable Search Engine if you don't want to use Google Search you can just type the url of the Search Engine you do want to use in the address bar.

HummingBird has no AI at all and is also dseigned to be Simpler to use then Google Chrome and other Web Browsers.

HummingBird Web Browser doesn't track you or Sell your Data. Because HummingBird is a Web Browser not an advertising Platform.

Currently HummingBird Web Browser only works on Microsoft Windows however support for Linux is planned in the future.
